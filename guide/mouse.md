# Mouse

Cub has full mouse support — you never *need* it, but it's there when you want
it.

## Editor

- **Click** to place the cursor.
- **Drag** to select text.
- **Scroll wheel** to move through the buffer.

## Tabs

- **Click** a tab to switch to it.
- **Click its `✕`** to close it (a dirty tab warns first — see [Tabs](./tabs)).

## Sidebar

- **Click** an entry to open a file or toggle a folder.
- **Drag the border** (`│`) to resize the sidebar.
- **Scroll** over the sidebar to move through the file tree.

## Terminal

- In the header, **click a numbered tab** to switch, its **`✕`** to close it, or
  **`+`** to open one.
- **Click the panel body** to focus it.
- **Drag the header bar** to resize the panel.
